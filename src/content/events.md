# YOUR EVENT DOES NOT START WHEN SOMEONE REGISTERS.

It starts when the right person discovers it.

From there, they need a reason to care, enough information to make a decision and a clear path to registration. Then comes attendance, engagement and what happens after the event.

**Discovery → Interest → Registration → Attendance → What Comes Next**

### Discovery

Get the event in front of the right people through search, social media, content and campaigns.

### Interest

Give people a clear reason to care, with the message, information and proof they need to see the event as relevant to them.

### Registration

Make the next step simple. The right person should not have to search for where to register, figure out what to do next or work through unnecessary friction.

### Attendance

Keep people informed and engaged between registration and the event so interest does not disappear after the sign-up.

### What Comes Next

The value of an event does not have to end when the event does. The relationships, attention and opportunities created can continue into the next conversation, offer or event.

# YOUR DIGITAL PRESENCE NEEDS TO DO MORE THAN PROMOTE.

Promotion can get people to notice an event.

But attention alone does not create registrations or attendance.

Your digital presence needs to help the right person move from **“I’ve seen this”** to **“I want to be there.”**

### ATTRACT

Put the event in front of people who are actually likely to care.

That can mean search visibility, social content, campaigns, partnerships and other channels that bring the right audience into the journey.

### ENGAGE

Give people enough reason to stop, understand the event and see why it matters to them.

The message, content, event information and proof all have a role here.

### CONVERT

Make the decision and next step straightforward.

Whether that means registering, buying a ticket, submitting an enquiry or joining a waitlist, the path should be clear and easy to complete.

### MEASURE

Know what is actually producing interest and action.

Track the actions that matter so you can see where people are coming from, what they respond to and where potential registrations are being lost.

### EXTEND

Keep the relationship moving after the event.

Follow-up, content, future events and other relevant opportunities can turn one interaction into a longer-term relationship.

The right combination depends on the event, the audience and what you want the event to achieve.
