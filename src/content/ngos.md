# YOUR WORK CAN BE MEANINGFUL AND STILL BE HARD TO FIND.

Your organization may already be doing important work.

Running programmes.
Supporting communities.
Publishing research.
Creating change.
Working with partners.
Delivering impact.

But if the right people cannot easily find, understand or trust that work online, some of that value stays invisible.

Your digital presence should help the right people discover what you do, understand why it matters and take the next step.

**Make Your Work Easier to Find**

# PEOPLE NEED MORE THAN PROOF THAT YOU EXIST.

They need to understand what you do, why it matters and what they can do next.

For an NGO or NPO, that journey can involve donors, partners, funders, beneficiaries, volunteers, institutions, researchers, media and other stakeholders.

Your digital presence needs to support that journey instead of making each person figure it out for themselves.

### DISCOVERY

Help the right people find your organization through search, social media, content, campaigns, partnerships and other relevant channels.

### UNDERSTANDING

Make your work easy to understand.

Your programmes, mission, impact, expertise and opportunities should be communicated clearly enough for someone unfamiliar with the organization to understand where they fit.

### TRUST

Give people enough evidence to believe in the work.

Programme information, impact stories, reports, partnerships, people, projects and other proof can help turn interest into confidence.

### ACTION

Make the next step obvious.

Whether someone wants to donate, partner, enquire, volunteer, attend a programme, download a report or get involved, they should know what to do.

### CONTINUATION

Keep the relationship moving.

The person who discovered your organization today may become a donor, partner, participant, advocate or supporter tomorrow.

The digital experience should make that progression easier.

# YOUR DIGITAL PRESENCE NEEDS TO DO MORE THAN EXPLAIN WHAT YOU DO.

A website can tell someone your organization exists.

A social media page can show that you are active.

But neither automatically helps the right person understand where they fit.

Your digital presence should help your organization move people from awareness to action.

### ATTRACT

Make the work easier for the right people to discover.

Search visibility, social content, campaigns, partnerships and useful resources can bring relevant audiences into the journey.

### ENGAGE

Give people a reason to stay.

Clear programme information, useful content, stories, research and relevant updates can help people understand the work beyond a single post or page.

### CONVERT

Make the next step clear.

A donor, partner, volunteer, participant or organization should not have to search through the website to figure out how to get involved.

### MEASURE

Understand what is actually creating engagement.

Track meaningful actions so you can see which channels, pages and campaigns are contributing to enquiries, registrations, downloads, donations or other important outcomes.

### EXTEND

Keep valuable relationships moving.

Follow-up content, campaigns, reports, events, programmes and future opportunities can turn one interaction into continued engagement.

Your organization does not need every possible digital channel.

It needs the right system for what you are trying to achieve.

# QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

## Still figuring out what your organization needs?

### Can you work with an organization that already has a website?

Yes. An existing website can often become part of a stronger digital journey without rebuilding everything from scratch.

### Do we need a full marketing system?

Not necessarily.

The right starting point depends on what you are trying to achieve and where the biggest gap currently sits.

### Can you work with organizations that already have an active social media presence?

Yes.

An active social presence is useful, but the important question is whether it is helping the organization reach the right people and move them toward meaningful action.

### Can you help us communicate complex programmes or research?

Yes.

The goal is to make complex work easier to understand without removing the substance that makes it valuable.

### Can you help us reach donors and partners?

The digital system can be structured around the audiences and actions that matter to the organization, including donor, partner and stakeholder journeys.

### How do we know what is actually working?

The right conversion tracking can connect digital activity to meaningful actions such as enquiries, registrations, downloads, applications, donations or other outcomes relevant to the organization.

### Can you work with an existing team?

Yes.

The system can be built around the people, resources and channels the organization already has instead of assuming everything needs to be replaced.
