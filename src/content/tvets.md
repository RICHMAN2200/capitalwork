They are trying to answer a much bigger question:

**“Is this the right place for me?”**

**What courses do you offer?**

**What are the entry requirements?**

**How much does it cost?**

**When is the next intake?**

**Where are you located?**

**Is the institution recognized?**

**What can I do with this course?**

**How do I apply?**

Your digital presence should make those answers easy to find and easy to understand.

### ENQUIRY

# SECTION 06

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS



### Can you work with an institution that already has a website?

Yes. We can work with what already exists and identify what needs to be improved rather than rebuilding everything unnecessarily.

### Can you help us market individual courses?

Yes. Course specific pages, content, search visibility and campaigns can all be built around the courses and programmes you want to grow.

### Can you help us attract students for a new intake?

Yes. We can build the digital journey around an intake, from awareness and course discovery through enquiry and application.

### Can you help prospective students understand our courses better?

Yes. Course information should answer the questions students naturally have about requirements, fees, duration, location, intake dates, career relevance and how to apply.

### Can you help us generate enquiries through WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp can be connected to relevant parts of the student journey so prospective students have a direct way to ask questions and take the next step.

### Can you help us know which marketing is producing students?

We can set up tracking around meaningful actions such as calls, WhatsApp conversations, enquiries and applications so the institution has a clearer picture of what is contributing to results.

### Do we need to use every marketing channel?

No.

The right channels depend on what the institution is trying to achieve, who it needs to reach and where prospective students are already looking.
